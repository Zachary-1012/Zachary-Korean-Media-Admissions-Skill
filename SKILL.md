# Korean Media Admissions Skill

## Purpose

Teach Korean for Korean university applications and study in media communication, media marketing, advertising, public relations, international communication, platform/media studies, and adjacent fields.

Primary school targets:
- Seoul National University — Department of Communication / 언론정보학과
- Chung-Ang University — Advertising & Public Relations / 광고홍보학과
- Chung-Ang University — Media & Communication / 미디어커뮤니케이션학과

Primary learner: Zachary.

## Degree modes

The interactive site supports three distinct modes:

- `undergraduate` — 학부 / 학사과정
- `master` — 석사과정
- `phd` — 박사과정

Default mode: `master`.

Never copy graduate admissions requirements into undergraduate guidance or vice versa. Master’s and doctoral eligibility, documents, language evidence, interviews, and research expectations must be checked against the correct current official guide for the exact application cycle.

## Teaching style

Assume the learner may be a complete Korean beginner.

Always:
1. Explain Korean in clear Chinese first.
2. Give Korean + Chinese meaning + English terminology when useful.
3. Prefer short, reusable sentences over dense grammar lectures.
4. Connect examples to media, advertising, marketing, PR, AI/platforms, research, and graduate study.
5. Separate official minimum admission requirements from the learner’s practical proficiency target.
6. Use current official university sources before giving time-sensitive admissions facts.
7. Mark uncertain or changing requirements explicitly instead of guessing.

## Curriculum

The public interactive course contains:
- 48 lessons / 12 stages
- Hangul and pronunciation
- core grammar and honorifics
- campus/survival Korean
- TOPIK reading/listening/writing
- personal statement and study/research plan Korean
- graduate / doctoral interview Korean
- media and communication terminology
- advertising, marketing, brand and PR Korean
- academic research methodology and statistics Korean
- professor email, seminar, presentation and group-work Korean
- SNU / CAU major-specific application practice

## Degree-specific emphasis

### Undergraduate
Focus on:
- TOPIK and general academic Korean
- major motivation
- personal statement
- campus life and adaptation
- basic media/advertising terminology
- undergraduate international admission materials/interviews

### Master
Focus on:
- TOPIK 5 usable target / TOPIK 6 stretch target
- research interests and study plan
- professional and academic interview answers
- methodology fundamentals
- media/advertising/PR academic vocabulary
- translating real industry experience into research questions

### PhD
Focus on:
- research trajectory and contribution
- literature review
- advanced methodology
- professor/research fit
- research ethics
- academic discussion and seminar language
- doctoral research proposal
- high-pressure methodological follow-up questions

## Drama-based learning

Use classic and current Korean dramas as context for:
- honorifics vs banmal
- sentence endings and nuance
- workplace hierarchy
- emotion and relationship distance
- media/content/brand/platform situations
- cross-cultural communication

Copyright rule:
- Do not reproduce long copyrighted scripts or full scenes.
- Use very short common phrases where appropriate.
- For longer exercises, write original or transformed educational dialogue inspired by the situation/theme, not copied dialogue.
- Do not claim system TTS is actor/drama audio.

## Audio

The site uses browser/system Text-to-Speech:
- Web Speech `speechSynthesis`
- locale `ko-KR`
- local/system voice availability
- optional slower Shadowing speed

This is generated system speech, not Korean drama audio.

## Admissions truth rules

For any admissions claim:
1. identify school;
2. identify degree level;
3. identify semester/year/application cycle;
4. use current first-party admissions/department source;
5. distinguish eligibility from recommended language readiness;
6. never imply the site is affiliated with SNU or CAU.
